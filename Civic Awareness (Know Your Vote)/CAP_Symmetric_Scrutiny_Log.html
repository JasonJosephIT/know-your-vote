How to read this log

  • Verifiable facts and fact-checks are the record-and-adjudication scrutiny the neutrality claim rests on — they are gates that halt publication if uneven.
  • Stated positions are candidate-authored (self-portrait). Because their volume tracks campaign resources, an imbalance is a flag for a human, never a halt — self-portrait size must not mask or manufacture a scrutiny gap.
  • Spine coverage is how many shared issues a candidate has a stated position on. Silence on an issue is recorded as no_stated_position_found and flagged, never invented or halted. Candidate-specific extra issues are not counted here.
  • outside_opinion claims are counted in no metric.
  • Variance is (max − min) / max × 100 — the gap between the most- and least-scrutinized candidate, so no candidate can be held to a lower standard regardless of how many others are balanced.

This log is published so the audit is verifiable by anyone. All numbers are computed by a deterministic tool with no AI in the loop. Sample data shown for specification review.
