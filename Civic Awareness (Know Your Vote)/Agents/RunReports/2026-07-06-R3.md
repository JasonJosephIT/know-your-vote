## 2. key_dates verification — NO MISMATCH FOUND

Cross-checked `race.key_dates` against dos.fl.gov (Election Dates / Vote-by-Mail pages,
Tier 1) and the three reachable county SoE pages:

| Field | DB value (all 9 races) | Official value | Match? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary election day | 2026-08-18 | August 18, 2026 | Yes |
| General election day | 2026-11-03 | November 3, 2026 | Yes |
| General registration deadline | 2026-10-05 | October 5, 2026 | Yes |

Structural note (not a mismatch): `key_dates` stores a single `registration_deadline`
(2026-10-05, the *general* deadline). The *primary*-cycle registration deadline
(July 20, 2026, per dos.fl.gov, Miami-Dade, and Hillsborough) has no DB field. The one
field the DB has is correct. This is a data-model gap for the product team, **not** an
official-date-differs-from-DB flag. **Nothing to flag for the R2 gate this run.**
