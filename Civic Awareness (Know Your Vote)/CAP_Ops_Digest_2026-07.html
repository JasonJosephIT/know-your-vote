Either the cron has never fired since these 8 races went live, or something is preventing the insert from landing — worth checking Vercel cron logs directly, since this digest cannot see execution logs, only the resulting table state.

Not a finding of balance — a finding of "not yet measurable," same root cause as the migration gate above.
