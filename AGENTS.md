## Neutrality

**Neutrality governs our conduct, not the world's.** We never introduce bias
through framing, selection, padding, or trimming. When coverage, opinion, or
attention is uneven *in the world*, we show it accurately, with its sources.
An imbalance is something to diagnose, not something to correct. Every
symmetric-coverage check asks one question: **did our pipeline miss something
(fix it), or is the skew real (show it)?**

1. Fix pipeline gaps only: missing feeds, name aliases, unequal search effort.
   Never pad a thin candidate or trim a heavy one to even out counts.
2. A real skew is shown with context ("14 sourced stories for A, 3 for B in the
   last 30 days"), not hidden and not editorialised.
3. Lean is still disclosed, never scored (`docs/general-election/news-fairness.md` §1).

Before calling a skew real, run the source check in
`docs/general-election/news-fairness.md` §2.
