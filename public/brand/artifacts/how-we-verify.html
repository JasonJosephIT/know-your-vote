Our method

How we verify

Every claim we show is sorted into one of three buckets before it reaches you. The bucket tells you what kind of thing you are reading — nothing more.

01 stated_position

What they say

A candidate’s own words — a quote, a bill they sponsored, a line from their platform. Presented as stated, attributed to where they said it.

02 verifiable_fact

What the record shows

A checkable fact — a roll-call vote, a public filing, an official dataset. Checked against a primary source and linked, so you can read it yourself.

03 outside_opinion

What others say

Analysis or commentary from a named third party — a court, an auditor, a rating group. Labeled as opinion and attributed, never presented as fact.

Where our facts come from

  • Official voting record — primary source
  • Public campaign filing — primary source
  • State elections dataset — primary source

Each verified claim links to its source. We describe what a claim is and where it comes from — we do not judge intent or motive.
