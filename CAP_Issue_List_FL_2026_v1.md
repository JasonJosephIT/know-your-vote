# CAP Issue List — Florida 2026 (Stage 1 output)

**Run date:** 2026-07-15
**Scope:** Florida statewide plus the four MVP districts (FL-10, FL-15, FL-23, FL-28)
**Cycle:** 2026
**Purpose:** The neutral issue set that drives Stage 2 ("Where They Stand"). Each `label` becomes an `{{ISSUE}}` value.

Two groups: issues specific to Florida, and national issues Florida voters weigh. Labels name topics, not positions. A candidate of any party can answer each scope question.

---

## How to read this

- **applies_to:** `voter_salience`, `office_power`, or `both`.
- **salience_note:** one neutral sentence on why the issue is in scope.
- **source_tier:** `university_or_primary` (defensible for issue selection) or `advocacy_or_partisan` (corroborating only, replace before locking). See §Sourcing notes.

## Group A — Florida-specific issues

**A1. Property insurance costs**
Scope question: Where the candidate stands on property insurance costs and regulation.
applies_to: both. Salience: Florida polling ranks property insurance among the top concerns for 2026 voters, and homeowners report premium increases over the past year.
Source: [AIF Q1 2025 statewide poll, via Florida Politics](https://floridapolitics.com/archives/723071-aif-poll-property-insurance-remains-voters-top-concern/) (advocacy_or_partisan). Corroborated by [UNF poll, News4Jax](https://www.news4jax.com/news/politics/2025/10/28/unf-poll-housing-costs-ranked-most-important-problem-facing-florida-voters-divided-over-eliminating-property-taxes/) (university_or_primary).

**A2. Housing affordability**
Scope question: Where the candidate stands on housing affordability and supply.
applies_to: voter_salience. Salience: A UNF poll placed housing costs as the most important problem facing Florida, ahead of insurance and property taxes.
Source: [UNF poll, News4Jax](https://www.news4jax.com/news/politics/2025/10/28/unf-poll-housing-costs-ranked-most-important-problem-facing-florida-voters-divided-over-eliminating-property-taxes/) (university_or_primary).

**A3. Property taxes**
Scope question: Where the candidate stands on property taxes and homestead exemptions.
applies_to: both. Salience: Multiple 2026 Florida ballot amendments address homestead exemptions and property assessments, and polling shows broad support for some form of property tax reform.
Source: [Florida 2026 ballot measures, Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Florida_2026_ballot_measures) (university_or_primary). Corroborated by [UNF poll](https://www.news4jax.com/news/politics/2025/10/28/unf-poll-housing-costs-ranked-most-important-problem-facing-florida-voters-divided-over-eliminating-property-taxes/).

**A4. Cost of living in Florida**
Scope question: Where the candidate stands on the cost of living for Florida households.
applies_to: voter_salience. Salience: A statewide poll reported that most Florida voters say their living costs rose, with insurance and taxes named as leading drivers.
Source: [Poll via Florida Politics](https://floridapolitics.com/archives/782499-poll-florida-voters-feel-cost-crunch-struggle-with-national-division/) (advocacy_or_partisan, replace with a university or primary poll before locking).

**A5. Water quality and Everglades restoration**
Scope question: Where the candidate stands on water quality, red tide, and Everglades restoration.
applies_to: both. Salience: Florida agencies and researchers track red tide severity and Everglades nutrient pollution, and the state runs an active Everglades restoration program.
Source: [2026 South Florida Environmental Report, SFWMD](https://www.sfwmd.gov/news-events/news/2026-south-florida-environmental-report-highlights-everglades-restoration-progress) (university_or_primary). Corroborated by [WGCU / PBS-NPR SW Florida](https://www.wgcu.org/environment/2026-04-17/red-tide-turning-point-new-research-policy-shifts-offer-hope).

**A6. Public education and school choice**
Scope question: Where the candidate stands on public school funding and the state voucher program.
applies_to: both. Salience: Florida's voucher program and public school funding are recurring subjects of state policy debate heading into 2026.
Source: [Florida elections 2026, Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Florida_elections,_2026) (university_or_primary). Note: the strongest coverage found was partisan messaging; replace with a vetted outlet before locking.

**A7. Elections administration and voting access**
Scope question: Where the candidate stands on election administration and voting access in Florida.
applies_to: both. Salience: Florida has certified and pending 2026 measures touching the initiative process and voting rights.
Source: [Florida Voting Rights Initiative 2026, Ballotpedia](https://ballotpedia.org/Florida_Voting_Rights_Initiative_(2026)) (university_or_primary).

## Balance check

The list carries issues that polling associates with both parties: property insurance, housing, cost of living, and water quality on the Florida side, and a national mix that pairs economy, immigration, and crime with healthcare, Social Security, abortion, and democracy. No issue is phrased as a position. Redraw the balance if a later edit adds a one-sided topic without its counterpart.

## Sourcing notes and limitations

- **Replace advocacy polls before locking.** A1 and A4 lean on AIF and a poll carried by Florida Politics. Use these as corroboration only. Ground issue selection on university or primary polling: UNF, USF, Stetson, AP-NORC, plus the Florida Division of Elections and Department of State for ballot certifications.
- **News sourcing gate still applies downstream.** When Stage 2 pulls Related News, restrict outlets to the two-gate list (AllSides Center and Ad Fontes green box). The salience sources here justify issue selection; they are not the Related News feed.
- **Ballot measures are moving.** Florida had two certified statewide measures as of March 2026, with property-tax proposals still in play through a possible special session. Re-pull ballot status before the brief goes live.
- **This is a versioned artifact.** Diff future runs against this file so issue changes stay visible.
