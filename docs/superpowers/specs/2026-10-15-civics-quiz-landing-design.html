"Do you know civics?": a simple quiz landing for the "Who's my ___?" ads

One short page that every ad links to: address first, then seven recall-and-reveal questions about who holds each office for you, every answer sourced, then on to the existing ballot guide. No product code here. Markdown version: 2026-10-15-civics-quiz-landing-design.md.

Every answer card has
  • The name, as the official source prints it
  • The office; "in office since"; elected or appointed, said for every holder
  • One sourced line on what the office does (never how well)
  • A receipt: Source · Where · Checked date
  • A ballot line: on your ballot (with a link), on your ballot but not covered, or not up this year
Stale guard: if a row was checked more than 14 days ago, or is past its changeover date, the card shows no name, only "We're re-checking this one. [Source] lists it."
Address box first

The same field and copy as the home page. The address goes from our server to Census only. It is never stored or logged, never put in a URL, and never sent to analytics or Google.

4. Question rules

  1. Facts only: who holds the office, since when, elected or appointed, what the office does, how many, what threshold, and whether it's on your ballot.
  2. Every answer is sourced with a receipt. No source means no name.
  3. Recall, not multiple choice: multiple choice would mean KYV picking which real politicians appear as the wrong answers.
  4. One template for every holder: no photos, and one party rule for everyone.
  1. Describe the office, never the holder.
  2. Amendments: process facts only, never whether they're good or bad.
  3. The ad's out-of-state politicians never appear on the page.
  4. No wordmark next to an answer card.
