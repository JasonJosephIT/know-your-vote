## What went wrong, and what was fixed

1. **`--limit` truncated silently.** It is now refused (`limitShortfall`, tested). The pilot's finding 2.
2. **Chromium in this container did not trust the proxy CA.** The browser store was empty, so every browser-fallback fetch failed with `net::ERR_CERT_AUTHORITY_INVALID`. Abrams and Jewett looked like bot walls on the first attempt. After the CA was added to the NSS store, both were re-run. Both then served real bot challenges that did not clear, so their silence is the site's, not ours. The ingest now logs the error message, not just `Error`. Both first attempts are kept in `attempt-1-failed/`.
3. **A visitor's comment was read as the candidate's words.** The Profiler review of Dimanche caught `0c289595`, a comment under his blog post, marked as a policy statement. The ingest now drops comment sections (`stripCommentSections`, tested). All five readable sites were re-ingested with the fix. Four came back identical apart from `retrieved_at`. Dimanche lost exactly that one passage and was re-run and re-reviewed (`reingest-2026-09-27.md`, `FL-DOE-88529/attempt-1-comment-leak/`).
4. **Datto, Abrams and Jewett could not enter the plan.** The writer now takes `"run": null` with a required `no_run_reason`, and gives each of them a profile and `no_stated_position_found` on every spine issue (tested).

## Reviewer findings for the founder (information only; nothing was hand-edited)

- **The commitment gate under-reports.** There are clear spine commitments just under 0.85:
  - Jolly: 11 possible misses, including "Here is the plan to cut them by 60 to 70 percent" at 0.80.
  - Russo: 1 clear miss and 5 borderline.
  - Burkett: 4 misses.
- **A1 "Property insurance costs" also catches health insurance.** Three of Burkett's six A1 passages sit in his health-care section. A claim filed under A1 from them would sit under the wrong topic. This is a question-wording problem in the Jev taxonomy (`tax-7`), not a run error.
- **The same sentence counts twice.** Donalds' A4 count of 2 is one sentence on two pages (one copy is in quotation marks, so the cross-page dedupe misses it).
- **Some passages that clear the gate have no commitment of their own:**
  - Burkett's Live Local Act criticism (A2);
  - Dimanche's HOA complaints (A3);
  - Jolly's lead-in lines.
- **The crawl reaches uneven depths.** Burkett's site produced 0 policy pages out of 47 links (homepage only), and Dimanche's produced 1 of 91. Jolly's hit the 8-page cap. No candidate's About page was fetched, so the bio section still has no source.
