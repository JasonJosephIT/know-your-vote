## How it reached the database

The Supabase MCP is the only path to the database. Each `<RACE>/brief.sql` (from `scripts/brief-rows-sql.ts`, unchanged) was split mechanically, at statement boundaries and in file order, by a quote-aware splitter. The pieces were packed into 38 calls of at most about 17 KB, and each call ran as its own transaction. Every race was `listed` throughout, so readers could not see a half-built brief. No going dark was needed.

**The 105 `DELETE` statements were left out.** Each brief starts with three: claims, positions and issues for its race.
- Before the apply, a read showed all 35 races had 0 claims, positions, issues and profiles. The deletes were therefore no-ops.
- Through the MCP, a `DELETE` hung until the 60-second timeout, even as a no-op and even with `SET statement_timeout = '25s'`. `INSERT` and `UPDATE` ran normally.
  - The first attempt (FL-10's batch, deletes included) timed out, and nothing from it landed.
  - The source row `src-daf9dbe2` (frostforcongress.com) came in early from a test insert. It is the same row the brief inserts, with `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`.
- A local PGlite database showed that the 38 calls without the deletes produce exactly the reference built from the unmodified `brief.sql` files. The reference is every repo migration, FL-GOV's live seed and brief, the roster, then the 35 briefs.

**One invisible character.** Claim `claim-FL-DOE-89522-f70a8906` (Haridopolos, FL-8) begins with a zero-width space (U+200B), as the ingest captured it from the site. A scan of all 38 calls found no other invisible or control character. That one literal was sent as `chr(8203) || ' Protecting …'` so the stored text is byte-identical. The fingerprint below confirms it.

## Proof that the database holds exactly the reviewed briefs

[`apply-fingerprint-35.sql`](apply-fingerprint-35.sql) computes five md5 hashes per race:
- claims: id, candidate, issue, text, bucket, attributed, verification, verdict;
- claim sources: claim, then url_norm, url, type;
- positions;
- issues;
- profiles, including the audit counts but not the three balance fields.

- **Partial check, mid-apply.** After call 24 of 38, the 14 races already complete (FL-7, FL-10 to FL-28) matched the reference on all five hashes. FL-8's claims, including the zero-width space, and its claim sources already matched too.
- **Full check.** After the last call, all 35 races matched the reference on all five hashes: **0 mismatches**. The hashes are in [`apply-fingerprints-2026-10-04.json`](apply-fingerprints-2026-10-04.json).

Every quote in the database is therefore byte-identical to the passage in the reviewed `brief.sql`.

## Rollback

- **Per race:** `set_race_publication(race_id, 'listed', 'founder', reason)` (0033) takes the brief down at once and keeps the roster. The brief rows can stay. Anon cannot read them at the listed tier.
- **To also remove the rows:** delete the race's claims, positions and issues, and its profiles. Run the deletes from a SQL session other than the MCP, because MCP `DELETE`s hang. Sources stay: the `source` table is shared across races.
