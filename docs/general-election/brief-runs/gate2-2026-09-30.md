# The second commitment gate (2026-09-30)

Founder, on the three options in `review-2026-09-29.md`: **"1"**, meaning add a second gate question to Jev, re-run Step 2 for every run, and re-review.

## The question

`src/lib/policy-noul.ts`, `OWN_COMMITMENT_ID` (`q_own_commitment`), asked in the same request as the existing gate and the 25 issue questions:

> Does this passage commit the candidate to something they will do, support, oppose, fund, or change? The candidate may be written about in the first person ("I will") or the third person ("she will", "he supports"), and a bare plan item ("Cut property taxes") counts. Answer no if the passage only gives biography, a past record or result, words or an endorsement from someone else, a description of a problem or of current conditions, or criticism of someone else, with no commitment by the candidate. …

A passage states a policy only if it clears **both** gates at the same 0.85 threshold as every other question. An unanswered gate counts as a no. This is tested in `scripts/verify-policy-noul.ts`.

## Pilot, before any run was redone

The new question was asked alone of two sets:
- the 70 passages the reviewers flagged under check 3;
- 146 policy passages from the same runs that no reviewer flagged, sampled deterministically by passage id.

Every answer is in `gate2-pilot-2026-09-30.jsonl`.

| Wording | Flagged still passing at 0.85 | Unflagged kept at 0.85 |
|---|---|---|
| First draft ("the speaker's own voice") | 2 / 70 | 50 / 146 |
| **Final (first or third person, plan items count)** | **18 / 70** | **105 / 146** |

The first draft rejected third-person campaign copy, which is how most campaign sites are written. For example, "Dr. Maxime will fight to raise funding and pay" scored 0.81 on it. The final wording fixes that.

Reading both error sides of the final wording:
- **Flagged but still passing:** several of these are real plan items the reviewers were strict about ("Expand I-4 …", "Make food, healthcare, and housing more affordable"). About 7 are genuine past-record lines ("Strengthened voter ID …", "$2M to expand …").
- **Unflagged but now rejected:** most are not commitments either (career summaries, descriptions of conditions, "Gus filed …"). A few real ones are lost ("Fix the insurance affordability crisis" scored 0.76).
