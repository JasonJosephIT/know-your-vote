**Preferred next combination:** a resident discussion, an independently recruited survey or public-feedback channel, and an official source explaining the decision. Service-request records add operational context. Several reposts of one news story provide discussion breadth, not independent factual confirmation.

## Social platforms with access limits

Public YouTube meeting recordings and published candidate forums can add understandable questions and answers. Jacksonville's archive already links recordings; speaker statements must be separated from independently verified facts. Content reuse requires separate rights review.

Public Facebook pages/groups, Instagram, TikTok and X may add useful discussion when a particular post and its comments are readable. No such comment corpus was analyzed here. Login barriers, private groups, algorithmic selection and unstable comment visibility make them less reproducible than public meeting records.

Nextdoor neighborhood feeds generally rely on accounts and neighborhood access. They are not an unrestricted open-data source. Use only genuinely public agency pages or published summaries where accessible; no private feed was accessed.

Do not join groups, solicit answers, submit surveys, file complaints or contact commenters merely to collect research. This task covers reading public material. Store aggregated questions and source links rather than personal identifiers.

## Collection template

For each new observation, record date, precise jurisdiction, platform, issue, the question being asked, original/news/survey/official-source role, access limits and shared-event cluster. Keep “reported experience,” “political opinion,” “verified rule/action” and “editorial inference” distinct.

For service requests, deduplicate cases, aggregate categories over comparable windows and note coverage gaps. Requests reflect reporting practices and service boundaries, not political support or an unbiased satisfaction rate. Remove unnecessary names, phone numbers and exact residential locations from working notes.

For surveys, record fieldwork dates, population, recruitment, sample size, weighting, question wording and sponsor where available. Do not combine percentages across different questionnaires or convert self-selected forum sentiment into a voter poll.
