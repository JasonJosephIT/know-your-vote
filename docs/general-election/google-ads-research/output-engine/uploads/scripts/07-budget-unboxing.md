## Fact card

- State exactly whether the item is proposed, voted, appropriated or implemented, with the date and the body.
- Never imply that one spending item caused another outcome (e.g. stadium money closing schools), and don't repeat anonymous misconduct claims.
- The binder label uses the real body name only when the record shown is real and current.
