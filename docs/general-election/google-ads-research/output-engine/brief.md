# KYV Florida Group Chat Top 10

## Objective

Focus on the top 10 from `../ranked-34.md` and give the Output Engine scripts and scenes it can
work from. Each video teaches one everyday civic question (who decides this? where did that
come from?) through a short character skit, and points to Know Your Vote or an official source.

## Audience

Florida voters in the four research regions: Tampa Bay, Miami/South Florida, Central Florida,
Jacksonville/Northeast Florida (`../regional-listening/README.md`). Regions are content
starting points, not targeting segments.

## Proposition

Before you guess, ask which office handles it and check the source.

## Mandatory elements

- The recurring cast and their fixed wardrobe: Ray, Jo, Mel, Dev
  (`../individual-video-sketches.md`, "The recurring cast and story").
- Neutrality (`AGENTS.md`, `../../google-ads-handoff-2026-10-13.md` §3). No party, candidate,
  real official or partisan colour. Jokes target guessing and overcomplication, never a party,
  official, community or group of voters.
- Every factual insert is sourced, current at export and posted in the caption. Never
  fabricate a KYV screen; show only live, verified product content.
- Brand: KYV Green and Cream lead; the funk palette supports (`docs/design.md`).
  Illustrated skin uses the six `illustration-skin` tokens only.
- No named business, utility or official is accused of anything. Proposals are labelled as
  proposals.
