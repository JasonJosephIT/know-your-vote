Root cause of the Miami-Dade gap

0032 loaded seats VoterFocus printed as (Active-Unopposed) / (Active-Elected). These eight print as (Inactive - Unopposed) / (Inactive - Elected): the winner closed the campaign account, which says nothing about the seat. Miami-Dade's three that 0032 did load (Bastien, Bendross-Mindingall, Colucci) were still Active; the other three counties had no Inactive winner in a covered office. parseVoterFocus (R2) reads the last status span, so the same read can't drop them again. Every other covered office on all four lists matches our rows.
