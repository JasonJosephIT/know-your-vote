## Bugs and findings

1. **The pilot's own prompt bug.** The first ingest prompt wrapped the command in `/usr/bin/time -v`, which this container lacks. It exited 127 before `node` started, so no request reached the site. The prompt is fixed and its revision history is recorded, and the failed attempt is kept in `attempt-1-failed/`.
2. **`candidate-policy-noul.ts` truncates silently at `--limit 200`.** The dry run showed "200 passage(s)" for a 215-passage file. A long site loses its tail and a short site loses nothing, so the cut is not equal treatment. The pilot passed `--limit 100000` to both the dry run and the real run, and every candidate must get the same flag. **Proposed fix (not made):** have the script exit non-zero, or at least warn, when `limit < passages`, in line with its fail-closed rule.
3. **The ingest page cap (8) was reached.** Jolly's site has 85 links. A site with more issue pages than the cap loses some of them, which is the same unevenness as bug 2 at the page level. It needs a decision: raise the cap uniformly, or record which pages were dropped.
4. **The ingest never fetches an About page.** `selectPolicyPages` follows only policy-looking links, so the **bio section's self-description** (D1) has no source today. Building the bio section needs the ingest to also take the About or "Who I am" page, under the same robots rules.
5. **Commitment-gate recall.** The reviewer found 11 passages marked "no policy" that plainly state a spine commitment. I spot-checked two and confirmed both. `5d9e0ab9` ("Floridians pay the highest home insurance prices in America. Here is the plan to cut them by 60 to 70 percent.") scores A1 0.94 but commitment 0.80, under the 0.85 gate. Five more catastrophe-fund passages pass the gate but score A1 only 0.47–0.80. The brief is therefore **conservative**: no false quotes, but it under-reports. A threshold change is a founder decision and would apply to every candidate. Nothing was hand-edited.
6. **`brief-rows-sql.ts` requires a `run` for every candidate.** Datto (D3) cannot go into `plan.json` without a small writer extension that emits a profile and `no_stated_position_found` for each spine issue. This is Step 4 work.
7. Noise: every script prints `MODULE_TYPELESS_PACKAGE_JSON`. Harmless.
8. **Jewett (D4) may not need the silence path.** `scottjewett.com` produced 389 passages on 2026-09-25, so the browser fallback got through then. Whether it still does will only be known when Step 1 runs.

## Evidence for D2 (`word_count`), which the founder deferred until now

The gate is `(max − min) / max ≤ 15%` over every candidate's `audit.word_count`, and `word_count` counts every claim, spine and candidate-tier.

- **Jolly:** 3,867 words (about 880 of them on the four spine issues; the rest are energy, health care, schools and his other issues).
- **Upper bounds for others:** the whole of Dimanche's site produced 1,057 words of passages and Donalds' 1,233 (2026-09-25 ingest). Even if every word became a claim, the variance is at least (3,867 − 1,057) / 3,867 = **73%**.
- **Datto:** 0 words under D3 → **variance 100%** on any site-only brief.

What this means for each option:

- **(b) A smaller spine cannot pass.** Datto sits at 0 on any spine.
- **(a) Recorder facts only pass if** every candidate's facts are long enough that Datto's facts alone come within 15% of Jolly's facts plus 3,867 words of positions. That is not realistic without padding, and padding breaks the writer's rules. Adding facts is still worth doing, because the bio section needs them anyway.
- **(c) A recorded threshold decision** is the only option that passes FL-GOV as it stands. It could raise `word_count_pct`, or narrow what `word_count` measures (for example spine claims plus facts, excluding candidate-tier). Either is a choice about our own fairness claim and must be written down as one.
