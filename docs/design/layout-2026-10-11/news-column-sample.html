Measurements

  • Ballot column fluid, up to about 760px; news column fixed at 340px; 48px gutter. Below 1024px it goes to one column.
  • Thumbnails are 4:3: 88×66 on desktop, 76×57 on phone. Radius 4px, always on the right.
  • Column heading in display type with a 2px ink rule under it, like a newspaper section rule.
  • Region filters are pills: ink fill when selected, white with a border otherwise.

What brand assets must fit

  • A no-image fallback state. About 2 in 3 rows have no photo, so the text-only row is the common case and needs to look intentional.
  • Optional: a small outlet-neutral glyph for official notices (Supervisor of Elections), in sage, not a logo.
  • The section rule and pill style should match the rest of the brand kit.

Never

  • No red or blue, no party colour, no lean colour anywhere in this column. Lean is disclosed only on each outlet's page.
  • No generated or stock images in the news slots. Only the publisher's own photo, or nothing.
  • No hand-picked "top story". The order is newest first, always.
