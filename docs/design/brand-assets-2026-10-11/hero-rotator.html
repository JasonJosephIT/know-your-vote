How it runs

Each walker holds for 2.4 s, then swaps in three beats: the leaving figure's forward streak (140 ms, nudged right), the arriving figure's trailing streak (140 ms, settling from the left), then the sharp figure. Only the walker changes; the sunburst stays still. With reduced motion on, the first walker stays put. Eight walkers: the phone reader, a nurse, a lineworker, a boat captain, a teacher, a student, a chef and a runner with a blade prosthetic.
