The plan in one paragraph

Phones get more pictures without adding any to the first screen, the lists or the frozen candidate pages. ABOUT
Who’s behind Know Your Vote
A free guide to Florida’s 2026 general election, run by Know Yours Inc, a nonprofit. No account, no paywall, no side.
What this is

Know Your Vote lays out Florida’s 2026 general election: the statewide races, the constitutional amendments, and the U.S. House and county races in Miami-Dade, Broward, Hillsborough, and Orange. In each race we brief, we quote what the candidates say about the issues, word for word from their own campaign websites, with a link to the page each quote came from. Where we found no position we could quote, we say so, which means we didn’t find one, not that the candidate has none. Some races list only who is on the ballot.

We never tell you who to vote for, never rank candidates, and never color-code parties.

How we stay fair spells out the whole method, including the checks a write-up has to pass before it goes up.

Who pays for it

Readers, through donations. No candidate, party, or campaign funds Know Your Vote or reviews what we publish.

Donations go through Zeffy, which charges us no fees. Know Yours Inc is a nonprofit but not a 501(c)(3), so donations are not tax-deductible.

Get in touch
Spotted a mistake, a missing source, or something that reads as slanted? Tell us. Corrections are the point. The same address works for press and everything else: info@knowyour.vote.
The official word
We’re not a government site. For your registration, your polling place, and your sample ballot, your county Supervisor of Elections and the Florida Division of Elections have the final say. If we ever disagree with them, they’re right.

The proposed design.md line

It replaces “Art on phones” in place, as one paragraph, so “Motion on phones” still follows it (verify-motion).

Art on phones (founder, 2026-10-11; extended 2026-10-14). Below lg, art is a closed list. Home: the key-dates sun (36px), the magnifier beside “On this page” (30px, the same piece at every width), a wave between the statewide races and the ballot questions (never inside either list), one seated reader on the zigzag after the ballot list (88px), the cat over the coverage card (64px), one still row of all eight hero walkers at one height standing on the wave after the coverage card (48px), the install-card badge (48px) and the reminder envelope (40px). Every page: the section dividers, and the footer town strip shown uncropped below sm from its 750px phone file (max 184px). Inner pages: one spot each on the 404 and the error page (120px, the same piece) and on /methodology (112px), plus a 30px magnifier beside /methodology’s “No source, no quote”; on /about, the same walker row on its own row after “What this is” and a 56px dog beside “Get in touch”. Each piece is static, lazy and fixed in px; it sits on an edge, on its own row or in a heading row after the words, and never under text. None go in lists, news, or race, candidate or measure pages, and the walker row never shares a screen with a candidate list. No motion below lg. Line icons drawn in Glyphs.tsx (20-unit grid, 1.75 stroke, currentColor, aria-hidden) are product UI, not art, at every width: they mark a voter’s own task (the sample-ballot tiles, the key-dates actions) or a prose page’s headings (/privacy), always beside words that say the same thing; they never mark a candidate, party, race, a measure’s YES/NO, a verdict or a news item, and they carry no tick, filled shape or ballot oval. Anything else on a phone needs a change to this line. The plans, sizes and budgets behind it are in docs/design/mobile-visuals-2026-10-11/ and docs/design/mobile-visuals-2026-10-14/.

Checks

Neutrality

  • Nothing is added in or beside a candidate list, ballot question, judges aside, news, verdict or YES/NO, or on any race, candidate, measure, news or outlet page.
  • The seam wave has no figure and looks the same for any set of races.
  • The walker row is eight figures of one height. It is ~2,800px from the statewide list, and its top is 723px below the end of the news, more than the 694px a phone screen shows. It never goes in the first screen, because FL-GOV has eight candidates.
  • Skin today is 1, 2, 2, 3, 4, 4, 5, 5, with no skin-6. Decision 4 fixes that before the row ships.
  • The icons are keyed by task, never by county. They have no ticks, ovals, handshake, or party or verdict colours, and every /privacy heading gets one.

Accessibility

  • All art is aria-hidden with alt="", and nothing new gets a tab stop. Icons are aria-hidden SVG.
  • Link names, heading names and the tiles’ sr-only text are unchanged (WCAG 2.5.3).
